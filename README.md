# JourneyIQ — AI-Powered Customer Journey Intelligence & Attribution Investigation
<img width="1781" height="838" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc9ea32b-efc5-4461-b8c7-964a11195f36" />

JourneyIQ is an AI-powered customer journey intelligence platform that helps marketers understand **what truly drives conversions**. By unifying fragmented customer interactions across multiple channels, JourneyIQ reconstructs complete customer journeys and provides explainable attribution insights that enable better marketing decisions.

## Problem Statement

Modern customers interact with brands across multiple touchpoints—advertisements, websites, email campaigns, mobile applications, and CRM systems. While businesses collect vast amounts of data from these channels, the data often exists in silos.

This creates major challenges:

* Fragmented customer journeys across platforms
* Conflicting attribution reports between analytics tools
* Difficulty measuring campaign effectiveness
* Poor visibility into conversion drivers
* Time-consuming manual report generation

As a result, marketers often struggle to answer a critical question:

**What actually caused this conversion?**

---

## Inspiration

Before building JourneyIQ, we researched discussions across Reddit, marketing communities, and industry blogs to understand the real pain points marketers face.

A recurring pattern emerged:

Marketers weren’t struggling with a lack of data—they were struggling with **too much disconnected data**.

Customer interactions were spread across multiple tools such as:

* Google Analytics
* Meta Ads
* Shopify
* Email Platforms
* CRM Systems

Different platforms frequently reported different conversion numbers, making it difficult for teams to explain performance, prove ROI, and optimize budgets confidently.

This insight inspired JourneyIQ.

---

## Our Solution

JourneyIQ transforms fragmented marketing data into a single, explainable customer journey.

Instead of acting as another analytics dashboard, JourneyIQ functions as an **AI-powered investigation layer** that:

* Unifies customer touchpoints across channels
* Reconstructs complete conversion journeys
* Detects attribution discrepancies
* Explains why conversions happened
* Generates actionable recommendations

JourneyIQ enables businesses to move beyond dashboards and toward explainable decision intelligence.

---

## Key Innovation — AI Attribution Investigator

The core innovation of JourneyIQ is the **AI Attribution Investigator**.

Traditional analytics tools answer:

* Where did the conversion happen?
* Which channel got credit?

JourneyIQ answers deeper questions:

* Why did the conversion happen?
* Which touchpoints influenced the customer most?
* Why are reporting tools showing conflicting numbers?
* Which campaign deserves more budget?

This transforms attribution from simple reporting into explainable AI-powered reasoning.

## How It Works

### 1. Data Ingestion

JourneyIQ collects customer interaction data from multiple marketing platforms.

### 2. Identity Resolution

Interactions from the same customer are linked across channels.

### 3. Journey Reconstruction

The platform builds a complete timeline from first touchpoint to conversion.

### 4. Attribution Analysis

AI evaluates channel influence and identifies discrepancies.

### 5. Insight Generation

Actionable explanations and recommendations are generated for marketers.
